全家一起记账，数据只在你自己手里

家庭记账是一款简单的记账 App。可以只在一台手机上用，也可以连接家里自己搭建的服务器，和家人共用一个账本。

记账与统计

  • 支出、收入、转账、退款和报销，几秒记一笔
  • 按月、按年统计，分类排行和每日趋势
  • 月度预算，超支提醒
  • 房贷、房租、工资等周期账目到期自动记账
  • 给账目附上小票、发票照片
  • 导出 CSV，可用 Excel 打开

全家共享（高级版）

  • 连接家庭服务器后，家人共用账本，多台手机自动同步
  • 没有网络时照常记账，联网后自动同步
  • 导入微信、支付宝账单，自动识别分类和去重
  • 一次购买，家中任一成员购买后全家可用

隐私

开发者没有服务器来接收你的数据，也看不到你的任何数据。账本只保存在你的手机和你自己的服务器上。App 没有广告，也没有统计分析等追踪工具。详见隐私政策。

Family bookkeeping where your data stays with you

Family Ledger is a simple bookkeeping app. Use it on a single phone, or connect it to a server you run at home and share one ledger with your family.

Bookkeeping and reports

  • Record expenses, income, transfers, refunds and reimbursements in seconds
  • Monthly and yearly reports with category rankings and daily trends
  • Monthly budgets with overspending alerts
  • Recurring items such as mortgage, rent and salary are recorded automatically when due
  • Attach photos of receipts and invoices
  • Export to CSV and open it in Excel

Family sharing (Premium)

  • Connect to your home server to share one ledger and sync across phones
  • Keep recording offline; changes sync when you’re back online
  • Import WeChat and Alipay bills with automatic categories and de-duplication
  • Buy once; when one family member buys, the whole family gets it

Privacy

The developer runs no server that receives your data and cannot see any of it. Your ledger lives only on your phone and your own server. No ads, no analytics or other tracking. See the privacy policy.
