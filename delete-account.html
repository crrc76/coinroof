删除账号与数据

本页说明如何删除你在“家庭记账”中的账号，以及删除后哪些数据会被清除、哪些会保留。

先了解：你的账号在哪里

“家庭记账”的账号建立在你家自己搭建的家庭服务器上，开发者没有任何服务器保存你的账号或账目。只在手机上使用、没有连接家庭服务器时，不存在账号。

在 App 内删除账号

  1. 打开“家庭记账”，进入 我的 → 账号与家庭。
  2. 点 删除账号，输入登录密码确认。
  3. 如需同时清除这台手机上的数据，勾选“同时清除这台手机上的数据”。

如果你是家中唯一的管理员，并且家里还有其他成员，请先在 我的 → 账号与家庭 → 家庭成员 中把另一位成员设为管理员，再删除自己的账号。

无法打开 App 时

  • 请家庭管理员在 我的 → 账号与家庭 → 家庭成员 中删除你的账号；或
  • 请管理家庭服务器的人在服务器上删除你的账号。

开发者不持有你的数据，无法替你查询或删除。如需帮助，可以联系：crrc76@163.com

删除哪些数据

数据处理方式
用户名、昵称、登录密码（哈希值）立即从家庭服务器删除，已登录的设备随即失效
高级版购买凭证立即从家庭服务器删除
你记过的账目和照片属于家庭共用的账本，保留给家人。你是家中最后一位成员时，服务器上的全部账目和照片会一并清空
手机上的数据勾选“同时清除这台手机上的数据”时立即清除；也可以直接卸载 App 清除

删除立即生效，开发者不另外保留任何副本。如果你的家庭服务器自己做了备份，备份由服务器管理者处理。

Deleting your account and data

This page explains how to delete your Family Ledger account, and which data is removed or kept.

Where your account lives

Family Ledger accounts are created on the home server your family runs. The developer runs no server that stores your account or records. If you use the app only on your phone without connecting to a home server, there is no account.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Family Ledger and go to Me → Account & family.
  2. Tap Delete account and enter your password to confirm.
  3. To also erase the data on this phone, tick the option to erase local data.

If you are the only admin and other members remain, first make another member an admin under Me → Account & family → Family members, then delete your account.

If you can’t open the app

  • Ask your family admin to delete your account under Me → Account & family → Family members, or
  • ask whoever manages your home server to delete your account on the server.

The developer does not hold your data and cannot look it up or delete it for you. For help, contact crrc76@163.com.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Username, display name, password (hash)Deleted from the home server immediately; signed-in devices are signed out
Premium purchase receiptDeleted from the home server immediately
Records and photos you addedThey belong to the shared family ledger and stay with your family. If you are the last member, all records and photos on the server are erased as well
Data on your phoneErased immediately if you tick the erase option; uninstalling the app also erases it

Deletion takes effect immediately and the developer keeps no copies. Backups made by your home server, if any, are handled by whoever manages it.
