部署家庭服务器

家庭服务器保存全家共用的账本，家人的手机通过它同步。它装在你家里一台常开的设备上，数据不经过开发者。

只是自己记账？不需要服务器。直接用 App 就行，数据保存在手机上。

要和家人共用账本、多台手机同步，才需要装家庭服务器。服务器免费；家庭共享需要高级版，家中任一成员购买后全家可用。

第一步：选一台设备

服务器要一直开着，家人才能随时同步（关机时大家照常记账，开机后自动补上）。按你家有的设备选：

群晖 NAS推荐本来就 24 小时开着，省电省心。 威联通 NAS推荐同上，用 Container Station 安装。 Windows 电脑下载一个程序，双击就能用。适合家里有台常开的台式机。 Mac 电脑下载、拖进“应用程序”，打开即用。Mac mini 很合适。 Linux / 树莓派用 Docker 一条命令启动。适合熟悉命令行的用户。

第二步：装好以后

连接手机、添加家人App 里点“自动查找”，找到服务器后创建账号。 在外面也能同步不在家时也想同步？用 Tailscale 安全地连回家。 备份与恢复数据都在一个文件夹里，复制一份就是备份。 升级服务器有新版本时 App 会提醒，升级不影响数据。

需要知道的几件事

  • 数据只在你家里。账目和照片保存在服务器设备上，以及每位家人的手机上。开发者没有任何服务器接收你的数据。
  • 手机和服务器要在同一个网络。默认只在家里的 WiFi 下同步；想在外面同步，看这篇。
  • 一台服务器对应一个家庭。第一个连接的人成为管理员，再由管理员添加家人。

Set up your home server

The home server keeps your family’s shared ledger, and everyone’s phone syncs through it. It runs on an always-on device in your home, and your data never passes through the developer.

Just tracking your own spending? You don’t need a server. Use the app on its own and your data stays on your phone.

To share one ledger with your family and sync across phones, set up a home server. The server is free; family sharing needs Premium, and when one family member buys it, everyone gets it.

Step 1: Pick a device

The server should stay on so your family can sync any time. (While it’s off, everyone can keep recording; changes sync once it’s back.) Choose based on what you have:

Synology NASRecommendedAlready on 24/7 and uses little power. QNAP NASRecommendedSame idea, installed with Container Station. Windows PCDownload one program and double-click it. Great for a desktop PC that stays on. MacDownload, drag into Applications, open. A Mac mini works well. Linux / Raspberry PiStart it with one Docker command. For people comfortable with a terminal.

Step 2: After it’s installed

Connect phones & familyTap “Find my home server” in the app, then create accounts. Sync away from homeUse Tailscale to reach home securely while you’re out. Backup & restoreAll data lives in one folder. Copy it and you have a backup. Updating the serverThe app tells you when there’s a new version. Updating keeps your data.

Good to know

  • Your data stays at home. Records and photos are stored on the server device and on each family member’s phone. The developer runs no server that receives your data.
  • Phones and the server must be on the same network. By default the app syncs on your home Wi‑Fi only. To sync while you’re out, see this guide.
  • One server, one family. The first person to connect becomes the admin and then adds the rest of the family.
