在 Mac 上安装

下载后拖进“应用程序”，打开就是家庭服务器。Mac mini 这类一直开着的 Mac 最合适。

  1. 看看你的 Mac 是哪种芯片

    点屏幕左上角的苹果菜单 → 关于本机。“芯片”一栏写着 Apple M1、M2…… 就选 Apple 芯片版；写着 Intel 就选 Intel 版。

  2. 下载

    Apple 芯片版Intel 版

    双击下载的 zip 解压，把 Coinroof Server 拖进“应用程序”文件夹。

  3. 第一次打开

    双击打开时，macOS 会提示无法验证开发者。这是因为程序还没有经过苹果公证，并不是病毒。这样处理：

    1. 先点“完成”关掉提示；
    2. 打开系统设置 → 隐私与安全性，拉到底部，在“已阻止打开 Coinroof Server”旁点“仍要打开”，输入电脑密码确认。

    如果之后提示“是否允许接受传入网络连接”，点“允许”。

  4. 看到“运行中”就成功了

    窗口里会显示运行中和一个地址（比如 192.168.1.10）。建议勾选开机自动启动和运行时不让电脑自动休眠。

  5. 连接手机

    打开手机上的 Coinroof，按连接手机操作。

日常使用

  • 关掉窗口不会停止服务器，它会缩到程序坞里。要真正关闭，点“退出服务器”。
  • 数据在哪：默认在“文稿/CoinroofServer”。窗口里点“打开文件夹”就能看到。
  • MacBook：“不让电脑自动休眠”在接着电源时有效；合上盖子仍会休眠，除非外接了显示器。
  • 有新版本时窗口里会提示，点“立即更新”即可，数据不受影响。

Install on a Mac

Download it, drag it into Applications, open it. That’s your home server. A Mac that stays on, like a Mac mini, is ideal.

  1. Check which chip your Mac has

    Click the Apple menu → About This Mac. If “Chip” says Apple M1, M2…, choose the Apple silicon version. If it says Intel, choose the Intel version.

  2. Download

    Apple siliconIntel

    Double-click the zip to unpack it, then drag Coinroof Server into your Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    macOS will say it can’t verify the developer. That’s because the app isn’t notarized by Apple yet; it isn’t malware. To open it:

    1. Click “Done” to close the message.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway next to “Coinroof Server was blocked”. Enter your password to confirm.

    If you’re asked whether to accept incoming network connections, click Allow.

  4. “Running” means it worked

    The window shows Running and an address (such as 192.168.1.10). We recommend turning on Start when I log in and Keep the computer awake.

  5. Connect your phone

    Open Coinroof on your phone and follow Connect phones & family.

Day to day

  • Closing the window doesn’t stop the server; it goes to the Dock. To stop it, click Quit server.
  • Where your data lives: by default in “Documents/CoinroofServer”. Click “Open folder” in the window to see it.
  • MacBooks: “Keep the computer awake” works while plugged in. Closing the lid still puts it to sleep unless an external display is connected.
  • When a new version is out, the window tells you. Click Update now; your data is kept.
