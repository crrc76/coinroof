隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-11-16

中文

“家庭记账”（以下简称“本应用”）重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用会处理哪些信息、存放在哪里，以及你如何管理这些信息。

一句话概括

你的记账数据只保存在你的手机上，以及你自己架设的家庭服务器上。开发者没有服务器来接收你的数据，也看不到你的任何数据。 本应用没有广告，不含统计分析或崩溃上报等第三方追踪工具。

本应用处理的信息

信息用途存放位置
收支记录、分类、账户、预算、周期记账规则记账功能你的手机；开启同步后，也存放在你的家庭服务器上
附加到账目的照片（小票、发票等）账目附件同上
家庭成员的用户名和密码登录家庭服务器、区分记账人家庭服务器（密码只保存不可逆的哈希值）；手机上只保存登录凭证
导入的微信、支付宝账单文件导入账单文件发送到你的家庭服务器解析，导入后只保留生成的账目
高级版购买凭证让同一家庭的成员共享高级版你的家庭服务器
应用锁密码打开应用前验证身份只保存在你的手机上（不可逆的哈希值），不会同步

权限

本应用只在你使用相应功能时调用以下能力：

  • 相机：拍摄小票、发票。照片只用于你正在记的那笔账。
  • 照片：通过系统的照片选择器选择你指定的照片。本应用无法读取相册中其他照片。
  • 面容 ID / 指纹：解锁应用锁。验证由系统完成，本应用拿不到你的生物特征信息。
  • 网络 / 本地网络：与你的家庭服务器同步。本应用只连接你在设置中填写的服务器地址。

第三方服务

  • 应用商店支付：通过 Google Play 或 App Store 购买高级版时，付款由商店处理，适用其各自的隐私政策。本应用不会接触你的支付信息。
  • 除此之外，本应用不向任何第三方发送数据。

数据传输安全

同步数据只在你的手机和你自己的服务器之间传输。如果服务器地址以 http:// 开头，数据在传输时不会加密，建议只在家庭局域网内使用，或为服务器配置 https://。

你的权利与数据删除

  • 删除单条数据：在应用内直接删除账目、照片、分类等。
  • 删除自己的账号：在“我的 → 账号与家庭 → 删除账号”中输入登录密码即可删除。账号信息（用户名、密码、购买凭证）会从服务器删除；你记过的账属于家庭共用的账本，会保留给家人。你是家庭中最后一位成员时，服务器上的全部账目和照片会一并清空。删除时可选择同时清除手机上的数据。
  • 删除家庭成员账号：家庭管理员也可以在“我的 → 账号与家庭 → 家庭成员”中删除其他成员。
  • 删除全部数据：卸载本应用会删除手机上的全部数据。服务器上的数据由服务器管理者（通常就是你或你的家人）自行删除。
  • 开发者不持有你的数据，因此无法替你查询、导出或删除数据。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

English

Family Ledger (“the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and how you control it.

In short

Your data is stored only on your phone and on the home server you set up yourself. The developer runs no server that receives your data and cannot see any of it. The app shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.

Information the app handles

InformationPurposeWhere it is stored
Income and expense records, categories, accounts, budgets, recurring rulesBookkeepingYour phone; also on your home server if you turn on sync
Photos attached to records (receipts, invoices)AttachmentsSame as above
Family members’ usernames and passwordsSigning in to your home server; showing who made each recordYour home server (passwords are stored only as one-way hashes); your phone keeps only a sign-in token
WeChat / Alipay bill files you importBill importSent to your home server to be read; only the resulting records are kept
Premium purchase receiptsSharing Premium with your familyYour home server
App lock PINVerifying it’s you before the app opensOnly on your phone (as a one-way hash), never synced

Permissions

The app uses these capabilities only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera: to photograph receipts and invoices. Photos are used only for the record you are editing.
  • Photos: to pick the photos you choose through the system photo picker. The app cannot read any other photos in your library.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: to unlock the app lock. The system performs the check; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Network / local network: to sync with your home server. The app connects only to the server address you enter.

Third-party services

  • App store payments: when you buy Premium through Google Play or the App Store, the store processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details.
  • The app sends no data to any other third party.

Security in transit

Synced data travels only between your phone and your own server. If the server address starts with http://, data is not encrypted in transit; we recommend using it only on your home network, or setting up https:// on your server.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete individual items: delete records, photos, categories and more directly in the app.
  • Delete your account: go to Me → Account & family → Delete account and enter your password. Your account details (username, password, purchase receipt) are removed from the server; records you added belong to the shared family ledger and stay with your family. If you are the last member of the family, all records and photos on the server are erased as well. You can also choose to erase the data on your phone.
  • Delete a family member’s account: the family admin can also delete other members under Me → Account & family → Family members.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app deletes all data on your phone. Data on the server is deleted by whoever manages the server (usually you or your family).
  • The developer does not hold your data, so we cannot look up, export or delete it for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
